## Description (paste as-is)
Exorcity turns every garbage spot in Bengaluru into a monster on a dark city map. The longer it stays uncleaned, the bigger and scarier it gets, so a scarier monster means a more urgent problem. Clean it with an after photo and the monster vanishes in smoke. Citizens and responsible societies, campuses and shops earn a Civic Score (0–900) with ratings and rewards.

Built for Kiroween 2026 (Costume Contest) with Kiro: steering docs, 9 specs with EARS requirements, agent hooks and property-based tests.
